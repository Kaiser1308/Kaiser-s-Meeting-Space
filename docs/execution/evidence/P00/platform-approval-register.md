# Infrastructure, Identity, Provider, and Cost Policy — Decision Register

**Phase:** P00 Design Closure  
**Created:** 2026-07-21  
**Status:** Design baseline for platform review  
**Owner:** Platform Engineering (register); individual owners per row

This register captures every infrastructure, identity, provider, and cost-policy decision that must be accepted before external beta. Each row represents a conservative engineering default that requires explicit approval from the designated owner at the listed release gate. Decisions marked **BLOCKED** cannot proceed without owner input.

Vendors remain replaceable behind accepted adapter contracts; no decision creates irrevocable vendor lock-in.

### Key gaps

1. **PLATFORM-012 (Residency/training review):** No provider terms review has been conducted for any provider. This is the single largest blocker — it gates PLATFORM-009, 010, and 011, and is co-dependent with PRIVACY-013.
2. **PLATFORM-014 (Budget caps):** No cost/budget controls or concurrency limits defined. Product must specify per-provider limits before external beta.
3. **PLATFORM-010/011 (Provider allowlists):** All generative AI and translation providers lack terms review, so their allowlists cannot be finalized for production.
4. **PLATFORM-002/005 (Region):** Singapore/APAC is the declared preference but is contingent on provider service availability — each service selection may require disclosure or legal approval for alternative regions.
5. **PLATFORM-016 (RPO/RTO):** Targets are planning aspirational only; no measurements exist to validate achievability.
6. **PLATFORM-015 (Account owners):** Named production account owner and signing identity owner need assignment with documented emergency procedures.
