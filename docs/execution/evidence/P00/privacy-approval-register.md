### Key gaps

1. **PRIVACY-001 (Consent copy):** No consent text exists. Product + Legal must provide and approve copy before beta.
2. **PRIVACY-009 (Telemetry allowlist):** No structured allowlist schema has been defined. Engineering must produce one before beta; Product must approve collection scope.
3. **PRIVACY-013 (Provider terms review):** No provider data-processing terms have been reviewed. This blocks Privacy-005 (provider deletion capability), Privacy-010 (jurisdiction), and Platform-level provider allowlists (PLATFORM-009 through 012).
4. **PRIVACY-003 / PRIVACY-015 (Retention):** The "indefinite" vs "30-day" conflict is resolved conceptually (personal retention vs recovery window) but the policy must be formally published.
